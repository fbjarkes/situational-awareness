# Definition

GDX is the US-listed VanEck Gold Miners ETF. Its objective is to follow,
before fees and expenses, the price and yield performance of the
MarketVector Global Gold Miners Index (MVGDXTR), covering gold-mining
companies.[^vaneck-overview] The US factsheet identifies NYSE Arca as the
exchange, USD as the currency, and 2006-05-16 as inception.[^vaneck-factsheet]
TradingView's symbol search resolves this instrument as `AMEX:GDX`.[^tradingview-symbol]

# Exposure and interpretation

This is equity exposure to mining businesses rather than direct bullion
ownership. VanEck describes miners as historically providing amplified
exposure to gold-price movements; that description is not a fixed leverage
multiple.[^vaneck-overview]

The issuer's 2026-10-22 holdings include Newmont, Agnico Eagle, Barrick,
Wheaton Precious Metals, and Franco-Nevada. Composition changes, so those
examples are dated context rather than a permanent constituent list.[^vaneck-overview]

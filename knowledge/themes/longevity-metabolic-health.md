# Membership criteria

Require documented products, services, revenue, or investment exposure relevant
to the scope. Marketing references alone do not establish membership. Record
exposure as core, diversified, or indirect, supported by a source and rationale.
Membership can overlap other themes and subthemes.

US-listed ADRs are eligible; foreign-only and OTC listings are excluded.
These classifications describe project relevance, not verified revenue thresholds
or investment merit. Core-universe role is separate from exposure concentration:
Lilly is classified diversified, while remaining a core selection. ALT is included
for metabolic liver disease rather than assumed to be a pure obesity developer.
Review dates do not establish historical membership; current provider symbol
resolution and clinical/commercial status must be checked before research.

# Longevity boundary and evidence gaps

The initial selection covers metabolic health and monitoring. It does not yet
populate healthy-ageing therapeutics or eldercare, and does not establish that
any selected company or treatment extends human lifespan. Record direct evidence
before adding longevity-specific claims. Separate established product businesses,
clinical-stage developers, and devices in peer comparisons; trial outcomes,
regulatory decisions, and financing can dominate developer returns.
No dedicated ETF proxy is selected. No report output or trading rule is enabled.

# Membership criteria

Require documented products, services, revenue, or investment exposure relevant
to the scope. Marketing references alone do not establish membership. Record
exposure as core, diversified, or indirect, supported by a source and rationale.
Membership can overlap other themes and subthemes.

The [shared membership table](/themes/theme-memberships.md) records six core
candidates: AVAV, KTOS, RCAT, MRCY, PLTR, and LHX. LMT, NOC, RTX, and GD
are four optional broader defense extensions. Only US exchange-listed stocks
are eligible; exclude OTC and foreign-only listings.

AeroVironment, Kratos, Red Cat, and Mercury are supported by their issuer
business descriptions.[^avav-issuer][^ktos-issuer][^rcat-issuer][^mrcy-issuer]
The remaining six names appear in SHLD holdings dated 2026-10-28.[^shld-holdings]
The shared table is authoritative for symbols, roles, review dates, and sources.
These are proposed project classifications, not an official industry membership
list or verified revenue thresholds. Palantir is diversified across commercial
and government customers; defense primes and smaller technology specialists
should be compared separately where appropriate. Company-level subthemes and
provider symbols require review before execution. Review dates do not establish
historical membership.

SHLD is a proposed ETF comparison proxy whose mandate includes defense software,
cybersecurity, and military systems.[^shld-holdings] Its global portfolio differs
from this US-only subset; the ETF is not itself a stock peer-group constituent.
Recording the shortlist does not enable report output or a trading rule.

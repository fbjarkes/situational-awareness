# Membership criteria

Require documented products, services, revenue, or investment exposure relevant
to the scope. Marketing references alone do not establish membership. Record
exposure as core, diversified, or indirect, supported by a source and rationale.
Membership can overlap other themes and subthemes.

The [shared membership table](/themes/theme-memberships.md) records ten initial
core candidates: ETN, PWR, HUBB, NVT, GEV, MYRG, ITRI, AMSC, WCC, and FLNC.
JCI and TSLA are two optional broader extensions. All twelve appear in GRID's
holdings dated 2026-10-28.[^grid-holdings]

Only US exchange-listed stocks are eligible, including US-listed foreign
issuers. Foreign-only and OTC listings are excluded. The shared table is
authoritative for provider-qualified symbols, roles, review dates, and sources.
Core versus extended and exposure labels are provisional project choices;
ETF inclusion does not verify a revenue threshold or company-level subtheme.
Company-specific exposure and symbol resolution remain pending review.

This custom selection is not a complete industry universe or a replication
of GRID. Equipment suppliers, engineering/construction firms, storage companies,
and broader industrial businesses may need separate peer groups. Review dates
do not establish historical membership. GRID is a proposed ETF comparison proxy;
its international holdings differ from this US-only basket, and the ETF is not
itself a stock peer-group member. No new report output or trading rule is enabled.

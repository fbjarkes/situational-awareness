Companies enabling new approaches to diagnosis, treatment, and healthcare delivery. This is a proposed project universe, not an industry classification
or a claim of current market leadership.

# Membership criteria

Require documented products, services, revenue, or investment exposure relevant
to the scope. Marketing references alone do not establish membership. Record
exposure as core, diversified, or indirect, supported by a source and rationale.
Membership can overlap other themes and subthemes.

The subtheme assignments and core/extended split are provisional project
judgments based on cited issuer descriptions, not official classifications or

an AI pure play, has proven treatment benefits, or has equivalent commercial
maturity. US exchange listings only; exclude OTC and foreign-only securities.
Review dates do not establish historical membership, and current provider
symbols/corporate actions must be checked before execution.

# Related themes and limitations

ISRG also appears in [Robotics](/themes/robotics-automation.md), and DXCM in
[Longevity and metabolic health](/themes/longevity-metabolic-health.md).
Preserve overlapping relationships and deduplicate within each parent universe.
Software/data licensing, testing reimbursement, clinical drug development,
care delivery, and device sales have different economics and event risks;
use subtheme peers as well as the broad basket for RS research.
Issuer product descriptions support proposed exposure, not independent
validation of clinical superiority, AI performance, or future equity returns.
No dedicated ETF proxy or new report output is selected.

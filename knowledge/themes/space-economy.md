# Security identity and corporate actions

SpaceX states that its IPO took place on 2026-06-12 and that its shares trade
under SPCX.[^spacex-listing] Nasdaq explicitly warns that the ticker previously
identified a different security. Do not join that old history to SpaceX prices;
verify issuer identity and start the SpaceX history at its own listing.[^spacex-history]
Its short available public history can prevent long-lookback research.

Rocket Lab's proposed Iridium acquisition remained pending in the cited September
2026 update, with completion expected in mid-2027 subject to approvals.[^iridium-acquisition]
Track both securities while independently listed, but check completion and
listing status before every research batch. Do not treat the planned combination
as already completed or forward-fill a delisted target. Record corporate-action
handling if evaluating historical peer groups.

Separate satellite operators, launch providers, Earth-observation/data companies,
and infrastructure suppliers for narrow RS comparisons. Redwire includes defense
activities; broader exposure is recorded as diversified even though it is a core
selection. Space and defense overlap, but membership in one does not automatically
create a second assignment. Use [Defense technology](/themes/defense-technology.md)

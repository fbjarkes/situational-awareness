# Membership criteria

Require documented products, services, revenue, or investment exposure relevant
to the scope. Marketing references alone do not establish membership. Record
exposure as core, diversified, or indirect, supported by a source and rationale.
Membership can overlap other themes and subthemes.

The [shared membership table](/themes/theme-memberships.md) records a US-listed
starting universe: ISRG (Intuitive Surgical), CGNX (Cognex), TER (Teradyne),
ROK (Rockwell Automation), and SYM (Symbotic). NVDA and TSLA are optional
broader ecosystem extensions. ISRG, CGNX, NVDA, and TSLA are supported by
BOTZ holdings dated 2026-10-28.[^botz-holdings] TER, ROK, and SYM are supported
by their issuer descriptions and US listing information.[^teradyne-ir][^rockwell-ir][^symbotic-ir]

Only US exchange listings are eligible, including US-listed foreign issuers or
ADRs; exclude OTC and foreign-only listings. The earlier Japanese and Swiss
listings have been removed. Core/extended and exposure labels are provisional
project judgments, not revenue thresholds. Subthemes remain pending review;
medical robotics and industrial automation may need separate peer groups.
Review dates do not establish historical membership.

BOTZ is a proposed ETF comparison proxy; its mandate covers robotics and AI,
including automation and autonomous vehicles.[^botz-holdings] The ETF is not a
stock peer-group member and includes a broader mix than this shortlist.
No new report output or RS calculation is enabled by recording the universe.

All are US-listed candidates, including foreign issuers and ADSs. Classifications
are provisional project judgments; review dates do not establish historical
membership. The shared table is authoritative for symbols and relationships.
JOBY has both air-taxi and autonomous-aviation rows; count it once when forming
the parent-theme universe. Its Drones membership is an extended relationship
based on autonomous-flight technology, not on the piloted eVTOL alone.

An eVTOL is not inherently unmanned. Separate piloted air taxis, autonomous
aviation, and passenger/cargo mission types in research; do not assume identical
certification, commercial readiness, or regulatory approvals across countries.
The candidates are peers by proposed application, not equivalent business maturity.
Vertical disclosed a NYSE minimum-price noncompliance notice in October 2026;
the notice had no immediate delisting effect, but current eligibility must be
checked before execution.[^vertical-listing] Eve describes itself as
pre-operational.[^eve-issuer] Provider symbols, adjustments, corporate actions,
and current listing status must be checked before research. No ETF proxy or
new report output is selected by this update.
